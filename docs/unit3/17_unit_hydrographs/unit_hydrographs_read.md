# Pre-Class - Unit Hydrographs

A unit hydrograph is the direct runoff hydrograph produced by one inch of rainfall excess falling uniformly over a watershed in a given duration. This lesson covers Sherman's definition and its assumptions, deriving a unit hydrograph from a measured storm, convolution to build a storm hydrograph from a unit hydrograph, and the lagging and S-curve methods for changing a unit hydrograph's duration.

## Reading
