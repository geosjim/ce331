# Pre-Class - Hydrologic Modeling and Review

This lesson pulls the rainfall-runoff pieces of the course together. It covers why engineers use hydrologic simulation models, how models are classified, the eight steps in watershed modeling, and the loss, transform, baseflow and routing computations that HEC-HMS performs for each subbasin. It ends with review problems and a single-basin WMS / HEC-HMS demonstration.

## Reading

Bedient, Huber & Vieux, *Hydrology and Floodplain Analysis*, 6th ed., Sections 5.1, 5.2, and 5.4 (pp. 306-312).
