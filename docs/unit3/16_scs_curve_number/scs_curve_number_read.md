# Pre-Class - SCS Curve Number

The SCS (NRCS) Curve Number method estimates the depth of direct runoff from the total storm rainfall using a single parameter, the curve number, that combines soil type and land use. This lesson covers the runoff equation, the potential maximum retention S and the initial abstraction, hydrologic soil groups, composite curve numbers, antecedent moisture adjustments, and how the method compares with the Horton and Green-Ampt infiltration models.

## Reading

### Question 1 - What the CN Method Is and Why Engineers Still Use It

In one or two sentences, describe what the SCS Curve Number method is designed to do, and explain why it became, and remains, the standard rainfall–runoff method in hydrologic design.

### Question 2 - The Runoff Equation, S, and Initial Abstraction

### Question 3 - Hydrologic Soil Groups and the Meaning of CN

Briefly describe what the four hydrologic soil groups (A, B, C, D) represent, and explain what physical condition is implied by CN ≈ 30 versus CN ≈ 100.

### Question 4 - Composite CN and AMC Adjustments
