# Pre-Class - Infiltration

Infiltration is the largest loss term in most storms, and it decides how much of the rainfall becomes runoff. This lesson covers the difference between infiltration capacity and the actual infiltration rate, Horton's empirical model of infiltration capacity decaying through a storm, and the physically based Green-Ampt method with its advancing wetting front and ponding time.

## Reading

Bedient, Huber & Vieux, *Hydrology and Floodplain Analysis*, 6th ed., Sections 2.7-2.8.
