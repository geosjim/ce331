# Example A - Lake Water Balance

A lake with a surface area of 1150 acres is monitored for one month (30 days). Over that month the average inflow to the lake is 30 cfs and the average outflow is 25 cfs. A seepage loss of 1.3 in. and an evaporation loss of 5.0 in. are measured, and the storage in the lake is observed to increase by 1.0 in. Use a water balance on the lake to estimate how much precipitation fell on the lake surface during the month.

**Given**

- Area A = 1150 ac
- Inflow I = 30 cfs
- Outflow O = 25 cfs
- Time t = 1 month (30 days)
- Seepage G = 1.3 in
- Evaporation E = 5.0 in
- Storage change ΔS = +1.0 in

**Find**

Precipitation P on the lake (in)

# Example B - Storm Water Balance on a Lot

A 7-acre paved lot receives 1.0 in/hr of rainfall steadily for 5 hours. During the storm, 0.15 in/hr of water evaporates from the surface, and a volume of 18 acre-inches accumulates as ponded water on the lot. The rest of the water leaves the lot as direct runoff. Assume there is no infiltration. Use a water balance to find the runoff, compute the runoff coefficient C, and then apply the Rational Method to estimate the peak flow rate.

**Given**

- Area A = 7 ac
- Intensity i = 1.0 in/hr
- Duration t = 5 hr
- Evaporation = 0.15 in/hr
- Ponded volume = 18 ac-in
- Infiltration = 0

**Find**

a. Total runoff R (in)<br>
b. Runoff coefficient C<br>
c. Peak flow Qp (cfs)
