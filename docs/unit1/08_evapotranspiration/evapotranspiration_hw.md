## Blaney-Criddle Background

Monthly consumptive use (potential crop ET):

$$ET_c = p\,(0.46\,T + 8)$$

Actual crop ET with crop coefficient:

$$ET = K_c \times ET_c$$

where:

- T = mean monthly air temperature (°C)
- p = monthly % of annual daylight hours (from tables)
- $K_c$ = crop coefficient
