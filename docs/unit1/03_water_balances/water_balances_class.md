# Example A - Rational Method on a Small Developed Site

A 4-acre commercial site receives 1.0 in/hr of rainfall steadily for 8 hours. During the storm, 0.05 in/hr of water evaporates from the surface, and a volume of 12 acre-inches accumulates as ponded water on the lot. The remainder leaves the lot as direct runoff. Assume there is no infiltration. Use a water balance to find the runoff coefficient C, and then apply the Rational Method to estimate the peak flow rate.

**Given**

- Area A = 4 acres
- Intensity i = 1.0 in/hr
- Duration = 8 hr
- Evaporation = 0.05 in/hr
- Ponded volume = 12 ac-in
- Infiltration = 0

**Find**

a. Runoff coefficient C (from the water balance)<br>
b. Peak flow Qp from Qp = CiA

# Example B - Annual Water Balance on a Large Basin

A 2000 km² watershed receives 80 cm of precipitation in a given year. The average rate of flow measured at the outlet of the watershed is 18 m³/s. Estimate the total water losses for the year (the combined effect of evaporation, transpiration, and groundwater outflow), and report how much runoff reached the river, expressed as a depth in cm over the watershed. Assume ΔS = 0.

**Given**

- A = 2000 km²
- Annual P = 80 cm
- Average outflow Q = 18 m³/s
- Time = 1 year

**Find**

a. Annual runoff depth R (cm)<br>
b. Annual losses (E + T + G) (cm)

# Example C - Runoff Coefficient from a Basin Water Balance

Using the result of Example B, where 80 cm of precipitation fell on a 2000 km² watershed and produced 28.4 cm of runoff at the outlet over the year, compute the basin's annual runoff coefficient, and give a brief explanation of the meaning of this coefficient here. This is the same C used in the Rational Method, but applied to a long-term annual water balance instead of a single design storm.

**Given**

- P = 80 cm
- R = 28.4 cm (from Example B)

**Find**

a. Runoff coefficient C = R/P<br>
b. Brief interpretation

# Example D - Monthly Water Balance, Find Outlet Flow

A 5 km² watershed receives 45 cm of precipitation over the course of a given month. During that same month, evaporation losses are 8 cm. Transpiration and groundwater losses are negligible and may be ignored. Find the average flow rate at the outlet of the watershed for that month, expressed in m³/s.

**Given**

- A = 5 km²
- Monthly P = 45 cm
- Monthly E = 8 cm
- T ≈ 0, G ≈ 0
- Time = 30 days

**Find**

a. Runoff depth R (cm)<br>
b. Average outlet flow Q (m³/s)
