# Pre-Class - Distributions

This lesson introduces statistical hydrology: why hydrologic variables such as annual peak flows are treated as random variables, how to summarize a record with histograms and frequency plots, how the sample moments (mean, standard deviation, skewness) describe a distribution, and the probability distributions commonly used in hydrology (normal, lognormal, Gumbel, Log-Pearson Type III).

## Reading

Bedient, Huber & Vieux, *Hydrology and Floodplain Analysis*, 6th ed., Sections 3.1 (Introduction), 3.2 (Probability Concepts), 3.3 (Random Variables and Probability Distributions), and 3.6.
