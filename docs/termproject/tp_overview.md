# Term Project - Water Supply Evaluation

The purpose of this project is to perform an analysis of the water supplies in Utah County. Your role is to identify data providers for hydrology and meteorology variables as well as demographic and consumption information at the national, state, and city government levels.

**Part 1:** You will obtain and analyze data to inform an engineering analysis of the current and projected state of the water supplies in Utah County. You will complete a series of short analyses of components of Utah County's water supply. The deliverables from these assessments will be compiled into an appendix for the report you will write in the second portion of the project.

**Part 2:** You will identify an intervention that could be used to improve Utah's water sustainability. You will do some analysis to assess the significance of interventions. You will then write a short report which describes the state of Utah's water resources, your identified problem and solution, and the technical analysis used to justify your recommendations.
