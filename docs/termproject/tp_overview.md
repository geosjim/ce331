# Term Project - Water Supply Evaluation

The purpose of this project is to perform an analysis of the water supplies in Utah County. Your role is to identify data providers for hydrology and meteorology variables as well as demographic and consumption information at the national, state, and city government levels. You will obtain and analyze data to inform an engineering analysis of the current and projected state of the water supplies in Utah County. You will complete a series of assignments which guide you to evaluate various aspects of the situation. In each assignment, you will produce a few tables and figures with explanatory text. These will culminate in producing an executive memo presenting a summary and recommendations which cites your work from the previous assignments as evidence of your conclusions.

Background slides: [water-supply-planning-presentations.pptx](water-supply-planning-presentations.pptx)
