# 1.4 Agricultural Water Use

## Introduction

The Census of Agriculture is conducted every 5 years and reports the irrigated acres harvested for each crop in each county. The Irrigation and Water Management Survey (IWMS) is conducted the year after each census and reports the average acre-feet of water applied per acre for each crop. The IWMS reports values for the state, not for counties. You can obtain both from NASS:

- [https://www.nass.usda.gov](https://www.nass.usda.gov){target='blank'}
- [https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov](https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov){target='blank'}

## Instructions

1. Get the irrigated acres harvested for each crop in Utah County from the 2022 Census of Agriculture using Quick Stats. Also get the total irrigated acres in Utah County.
2. Get the average acre-feet of water applied per acre for each crop in Utah from the 2023 IWMS. Also get the average for all irrigated land in Utah.
3. Estimate the volume of water applied to farms in Utah County. Multiply the irrigated acres of each crop by its acre-feet per acre and add the results. Apply the average for all irrigated land to any irrigated acres not reported by crop.
4. Convert the total to million gallons per day (MGD) and to gallons per capita per day using the 2025 Utah County population from [section 1.1](demographics.md). Compare these values with the public supply GPCD values from section 1.1.

## Deliverable

1. A table with the irrigated acres harvested, acre-feet of water applied per acre, and volume of water applied for each crop in Utah County. Include the irrigated acres not reported by crop and the totals.
2. The estimated volume of water applied to farms in Utah County in acre-feet per year, million gallons per day (MGD), and gallons per capita per day.
3. A comparison of these values with the public supply GPCD values from section 1.1.
