# 1.2 Rivers and Watersheds

## Instructions

1. Delineate the watersheds of the canyons in Utah County; namely Provo, Hobble Creek, Spanish Fork, Payson, and Santaquin canyons. Choose a spot to delineate near the mouth of the canyons. The specific outlet locations are not critical as long as they are near the mouth of the canyon. Save the basin boundary GeoJSONs.
2. Prepare one map of all the basin boundaries in ArcGIS/QGIS. Adjust colors and styles so the basins are distinguishable from each other.
3. Get gauge discharge from the USGS gauge 10150500 near the outlet of the Spanish Fork Canyon. To keep your analysis simpler and make plots readable, you may use daily average discharge since 1980-01-01. [https://waterdata.usgs.gov/nwis](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/nwis){target='blank'}
    1. Plot the hydrograph for the Spanish Fork Canyon gauge.
    2. Calculate the annual average discharge.
    3. Calculate the average discharge for each month.

## Deliverable

1. A map of the canyon watershed boundaries obtained from StreamStats.
    1. Each watershed outline should be visible.
    2. Add labels with the name of each watershed boundary.
2. Plot of the hydrograph from the Spanish Fork Canyon gauge since 1980.
