# Part 6 - Policy Actions

## Introduction

Much of water supply planning simplifies to a mass balance, or supply and demand. You need to have sufficient supply from multiple sources to match the current and projected consumption rates. If there is not a balance, you need new sources of water, lower supply, or both. New sources may not exist and are difficult and very slow to procure if they do. Consequently, most effort on water supply planning focuses on regulating or incentivizing lower consumption of water and reducing losses through increased efficiency.
